Cloning My Own Voice

11. Aug 2026 · 1 min read

A self-hosted app that learns your voice from a short sample and speaks any text back in it, built on top of Resemble AI's Chatterbox.

Project

I saw a video about Pocket TTS, a small local voice cloning model, and it reminded me of an old project I had wanted to build for a while. That got me back into working with speech synthesis, this time mimicking your own voice.
