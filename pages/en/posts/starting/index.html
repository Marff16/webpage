A short first note about what this page is for, why I want to write here, and what kind of posts I want to collect over time.

Meta Writing

This is the first post on this site, so it is mostly a marker or could be considered a small starting line for everything that might come after it.

I want this section to become a place for notes that are too specific for the front page and too useful to disappear in a private folder. Some posts will be technical, some will be reflective, and some will probably just be snapshots of what I am trying to understand at the time.

The goal is not to publish polished essays every time. The goal is to make thinking visible. I want a documentation of what I learned, what confused me, what changed after I built something, and what I would do differently next time.
