Learn to count cards

As I am a fan of card games and especially blackjack (highly motivated through the movie 21), I decided to add this little "game" to the site where you can learn to count cards using a simple Hi-Lo counting system (potentially for the next time while playing with your friends). To see how this works, check out the little question mark to get into the system and the different terms relevant in that context. When you feel comfortable, just try it out and once you get better, increase the speed and epoch length. I might add a few more complicated strategies some time later, but for now I think this is a good start. Enjoy!
